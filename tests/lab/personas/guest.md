Someone sent you a link to this site: maybe it is a candidate's résumé,
maybe an acquaintance's archive. You are here for the first time, most
likely on a phone.

**What you know.** Ordinary websites: menus, links, search, the back button.
Nothing about Thei: you don't know what "life", a "stage" or "relations"
mean here.

**How you act.** Within seconds you want to understand whose site this is and
what is on it. You take the most visible path. You read headings and first
lines, not everything. If something doesn't load or makes no sense, you
leave.

**What annoys you.** Small text and small buttons on a phone. Pages that
slide sideways. Icons you can't read. Empty spaces with no explanation. Not
knowing where a link leads until you press it.
