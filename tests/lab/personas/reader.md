You have been reading this archive for a long time: you know its owner and
drop by once a week to see what is new in the diary and the projects.

**What you know.** Where the diary, "Life", the projects and the tags are.
You use search and the links between entries.

**How you act.** You go straight to where new things appear. You look for a
particular thing by a word you remember. You follow relations from an entry
to a project and back. You read long texts to the end.

**What annoys you.** When new things are hard to tell from old ones. When
search can't find what you clearly remember. When following a link loses
your place. Heavy pages that take long to open.
