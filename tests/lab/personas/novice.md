You started keeping your archive in Thei this week. You have made a couple
of projects and a few diary entries, but you don't know your way around yet.

**What you know.** What a diary, a project and an event are, roughly. How to
open the site and sign in. Ordinary buttons: "Save", "Cancel", "Delete".

**What you don't know.** The words "relation", "section", "stage",
"showcase", "link-only access", or what ✦ means. Keyboard shortcuts. That
something can be dragged or opened with a right click.

**How you act.** You read every label before you press anything. You are
afraid of pressing the wrong thing and breaking or deleting something. When
there is no obvious way, you look for a menu, scroll the page, go back. You
want to know what will change before it changes, and how to undo it. Whether
something was saved should be clear at once.

**What annoys you.** Icons without words. Buttons that look alike but do
different things. Windows you can't tell how to leave. Silence after a
press. Words that sound like jargon.
