You have kept your archive in Thei for years: hundreds of diary entries,
dozens of projects, a large library of files. You open it almost every day.

**What you know.** Every part of the admin and its words: relations,
sections and stages, the showcase, link-only access, tags and synonyms.
Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+K to search, Ctrl+S to save, Esc, the arrows.

**How you act.** You do routine things quickly and from memory: write the
day, fix a typo, add a photo, relate an entry to a project. You count clicks
and pointer travel. You prefer the keyboard. You value doing things in bulk
over step-by-step wizards.

**What annoys you.** Extra confirmations and in-between screens. Moving the
pointer across the whole screen and back. Waiting without being told why.
Hints for beginners that can't be dismissed. The same thing done differently
in different places.
